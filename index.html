Costs Simulator

Estimate what running a liquidity pool could pay you. Pick a chain and token type and set the daily trading volume you expect - the chart shows your share of the pool's earnings after on-chain costs, and how much volume you need before you start making money. The revenue and gas-cost defaults come from real pools measured across several chains, and every input is adjustable - with finer controls tucked under each Advanced section.

These are estimates, not an offer. Defaults are calibrated as of September 2026 and will drift as chains and markets move.
